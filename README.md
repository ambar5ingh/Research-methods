# Research methods, visualised

An explorer of 56 research methods, written so that a beginner can follow it.

- **All methods**: cards grouped into seven families (experiments, clever comparisons, watching and counting, listening and understanding, mixing and doing, summing up what is known, models and data), with search and filters
- **Each method**: plain-words summary, time/cost/data/cause tiles, an emoji step-by-step, the research question and hypotheses (rewritten live for your own study), what you need, how to analyse it, what to watch out for, an example, and words to know
- **Help me choose**: up to three questions that lead to the methods that fit
- **Try the numbers**: a trial-size simulator and a risk calculator
- **Check a claim**: ten questions to ask of any statistical claim
- **Words**: a plain-language glossary, also shown through the small "i" buttons

Twelve designs have detailed diagrams and worked examples from David Spiegelhalter, *The Art of Statistics: Learning from Data* (Pelican, 2019). Other examples are well-known published studies or are marked "Illustration". Styling follows the India Agrivoltaics Dashboard.

Live site: https://ambar5ingh.github.io/Research-methods/

## Running locally

It is a static site with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser.

- `assets/data.js` holds the twelve book-based designs
- `assets/more.js` holds the other methods, the chooser tree and the glossary
- `assets/diagrams.js` draws the SVG diagrams
- `assets/app.js` handles the interface and calculators
